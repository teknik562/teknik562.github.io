Kilometrix

A simple, private Android app for logging your electric car’s charging, real-world consumption and costs. It works with any electric car, and you can have more than one vehicle. Tesla owners can optionally connect their Tesla account and fetch charging data with one tap.

Get it on Google Play

Your data

Your charge log stays on your phone. You can back it up to your own Google Drive; the app can only see its own backup there. Our server only relays Tesla requests and keeps what is needed for your account and usage limits.
